# We're Going on a Goon Hunt 🎃👻

A spooky (but not too spooky) tap-along story game for kids ages 3–6, inspired by the picture book *We're Going on a Goon Hunt* by Michael Rex.

## Play

- **Online:** https://awaxfc.github.io/goon-hunt/
- **Offline:** download `index.html` and open it in any browser. No install needed.

## How to play

1. Hold the phone upright and turn the volume up (on iPhone, turn off the silent switch).
2. Tap **Start the Hunt!** Sound starts after the first tap.
3. Shout along with the chant, tap **Over?** and **Under?**, then **Through!** to get past each place.
4. Find the hidden monster in every scene.
5. Meet the Goon... then run home! At the end, tap **Hunt Again!** to replay.

A full play takes about 5–8 minutes. All art and sound are made in code, so it works offline.
